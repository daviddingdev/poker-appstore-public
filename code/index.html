About

Poker Log

Log the hands that mattered in seconds, then drill the spots you got wrong. It works entirely on this device — there is no account, no server and no analytics, and nothing you log is ever uploaded anywhere.

What the drill answers are. Preflop push/fold comes from an approximate-Nash solve; postflop is a heuristic engine calibrated by simulation. Both are checked against brute-force equity, and spots near a decision boundary are shown as a mix rather than a right answer. It is a strong practice tool, not a full postflop solver — when a spot really matters, verify it in one.
