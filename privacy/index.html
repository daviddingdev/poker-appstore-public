Privacy Policy

Pocket Tells · Effective 24 August 2026

The short version: we collect nothing. Pocket Tells has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and makes no network connections. Everything you enter stays on your device, under your control, full stop.

What the app collects

Nothing. Pocket Tells does not collect, transmit, store remotely, share, or sell any data — personal or otherwise. There is no server to send anything to.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — hands, sessions, tournament results, drill answers, and preferences — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it (which is why the app encourages you to keep a backup file).

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings. That file is created on your device and handed to you through the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes — Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email — is entirely your choice and happens under the privacy terms of whatever service you send it to. The app itself never sends it anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that transmit data
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information about you from the purchase. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is a study tool involving simulated poker with no wagering and no real-money play. It is rated for adults, and since it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices ever change, this page will be updated before the change ships, with the effective date above revised. Given the app's design — no server, no network code — a change in practice would mean a change in what the app fundamentally is, and we have no intention of making one.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy can be raised on the project's public issue tracker. A dedicated support email address will be listed here when the app reaches the App Store.

Pocket Tells is an offline-first iOS app. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard web-server access data (such as IP addresses) when you view it, per GitHub's privacy statement. The app itself never talks to this or any other website.
