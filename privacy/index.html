Privacy Policy

Pocket Tells · Last updated 5 October 2026

Pocket Tells collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking — the app does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter — hands, sessions, tournaments, home-game nights, drill answers and settings — is stored only on your iPhone:

  • in the app’s own storage, and
  • as copies in the app’s Documents folder, so that iOS clearing web storage can’t lose it. The rolling autobackup files are visible to you in the Files app under Pocket Tells › pokerlog.

Nobody else — including the developer — can see any of it.

What leaves your phone, and only when you say so

  • Backup files. “Save backup file” writes a file and opens the share sheet. The file goes wherever you send it. It is plain text holding all your data, so treat it like any personal document.
  • Copy and share. Copying a hand, or sharing a home game’s payments, sends exactly the text you see in the share sheet, to whoever you choose.
  • Report a problem. If you use it, your Mail app opens with an email for you to read, edit and send — or not.

Buying Pro

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple ID; it never sees your name, email or payment details. The developer receives Apple’s standard sales reports, which don’t identify you. Apple’s handling of the purchase is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Apple’s own settings

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with developers. That is Apple’s setting, off unless you turned it on, and it never includes anything you logged.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all data removes everything in the app. Deleting the app removes its storage and its Documents folder. Backup files you exported stay wherever you put them.

Children

The app is a logging and study tool for poker with no wagering and no real-money play. It is rated for adults, and since it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page and the App Store privacy label change first. Questions: email pockettells@gmail.com, or see the support page.
