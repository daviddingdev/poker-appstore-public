Pocket Tells Support

Settle your home game, log the hands that mattered, and learn what wins. For iPhone.

Email pockettells@gmail.com with a question, a bug or an idea. If something isn’t working, say what you did and what you saw, and add your iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps. Please don’t send your backup file unless we ask — it holds everything you’ve logged.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so nobody — including us — can see it or recover it for you. Keep a backup: Settings › Save backup file. The app also keeps rolling autobackup copies you can see in the Files app under Pocket Tells › pokerlog.

Moving to a new iPhone

On the old phone, Settings › Save backup file and send the file to yourself (AirDrop, Files or email). On the new phone, Settings › Restore from a backup file. Then bring Pro back with Restore, below.

I bought Pro but it’s locked

Open Settings › See what Pro adds and tap Restore purchase, or use Settings › About › Restore purchases. Use the Apple ID you bought it with. Pro is a one-time purchase, never a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles every purchase and refund. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Pocket Tells collects nothing. The details are in the privacy policy.
